Citation CJ2, CJ3 and CJ4 Overview

The Citation CJ family is the most successful line of light jets ever built, tracing its roots to the original CitationJet of 1993 and growing into the largest installed base of any light jet. The CJ2, CJ3, and CJ4 are three distinct models within that family – each progressively larger and more capable, and each its own aircraft rather than a variant of the others – united by a shared lineage, Williams FJ44 engines, and single-pilot certification that has made the series a favorite of owner-operators and charter fleets alike.

The Citation CJ2 is the compact, efficient member of the family. In its current CJ2+ form it is powered by Williams FJ44-3A-24 engines, seats around six passengers, and offers a range of roughly 1,600 nautical miles, all at some of the lowest operating costs in the class. The Citation CJ3 is the sweet spot of the family: a longer cabin, Williams FJ44-3A engines with a long 4,000-hour time between overhauls, and a range of about 2,000 nautical miles that reaches across nearly all of the United States. The current CJ3+ adds Garmin G3000 avionics, and the aircraft’s blend of range, economy, and cabin comfort has made it one of the most sought-after light jets on the market.

The Citation CJ4 is the flagship of the line. Introduced in 2010 and now offered as the CJ4 Gen2, it is the largest and fastest of the three, with a moderately swept wing derived from the larger Citation Sovereign, more powerful Williams FJ44-4A engines, a range of about 2,165 nautical miles, and seating for up to ten. Across all three, Textron’s global service and parts network is among the deepest in business aviation – and while the CJ2 is no longer in production, the CJ3 and CJ4 lines continue today. That combination of broad market demand, extensive factory support, and efficient operating economics is exactly what makes professional management so valuable.

Sustainability

OUR COMMITMENT

At Capital Jets, we are committed to sustainability and providing eco-friendly alternatives for discerning passengers. We’ve implemented practices that have made strides in reducing and improving our carbon footprint, and are always turning to emerging technologies to further those efforts. By doing so, we’re improving the quality of our operations and the experience of our clients— setting the standard for others to follow.

In addition, we prioritize eco-friendly practices throughout our operations, such as double-sided printing and recycling.

RESPONSIBLE AVIATION

Conserve energy by enabling sleep mode on devices whenever possible.
Ensure office electronics charge during off-peak hours
Incorporate motion-activated lighting to reduce energy waste.
Decrease landfill waste by providing reusable water bottles rather than single-use plastic
Reduce e-waste by donating electronics to extend their useful life.
Limit commuting-related emissions and energy use by allowing employees to telecommute when practical.
Support local, eco-friendly vendors to reduce the environmental impact of your supply chain.

CARBON NEUTRAL FLIGHTS

In an effort to reduce the environmental impact of all our flights, Capital Jets participates in the carbon market by offsetting all our emissions. By investing in carbon credits and renewable energy projects, Capital Jets has been able to balance out the greenhouse gas emissions produced by our aircraft. Through these avenues of investment, we’ve effectively neutralized the greenhouse gas emissions generated by our aircraft. This unwavering commitment to sustainability not only benefits the environment, but also highlights what
our priorities are.

Services

COMPREHENSIVE SERVICES DESIGNED FOR YOUR NEEDS

At Capital Jets, we specialize in providing expert aircraft services designed to meet the unique needs of private jet owners. From overseeing the day-to-day operations and maintenance of your aircraft to guiding you through the process of acquiring the perfect jet, our team delivers unmatched expertise and personalized attention.

AIRCRAFT MANAGEMENT

We understand that owning a private aircraft is reflective of success but also a significant investment. That’s why we offer comprehensive aircraft management services that cater to the unique needs and requirements of aircraft owners. Leveraging our team’s expertise and dedication, we strive to provide you with a seamless and worry-free ownership experience.

Our team of seasoned professionals are cohesively tasked with overseeing all aspects of your aircraft’s operations, from maintenance and scheduling to crew management and regulatory compliance. We meticulously handle the intricate details, ensuring that your aircraft is meticulously maintained, fully operational, and always ready for your next flight.

AIRCRAFT ACQUISITION

If you’re interested in buying an aircraft and having us manage it, we can assist you throughout the entire process from beginning to end. We’ll work with you to search for the right aircraft based on your needs, perform pre-buying inspections, conduct a review of all records, and negotiate on your behalf. Ultimately, you’ll have the reassurance that your purchase was thoroughly vetted, your aircraft will be well-maintained and your value protected.

Embraer Phenom 300 Overview

The Phenom 300 is the best-selling light jet in the world, a distinction it has held for well over a decade. Certified in 2009 as a larger companion to Embraer’s Phenom 100, it combined performance, cabin comfort, and modern avionics in a way that quickly made it the benchmark of the light-jet class – and it has outsold every competitor in its segment year after year, with more than 700 delivered – the market leader in the light-jet class.

The current production standard is the Phenom 300E, an enhanced version introduced in 2018 and further upgraded in 2020 with more powerful Pratt & Whitney Canada PW535E1 engines, a longer range of about 2,010 nautical miles, and a top speed of Mach 0.80. It flies behind the Garmin-based Prodigy Touch flight deck, cruises as high as 45,000 feet, and was the first business jet to introduce a runway overrun awareness and alerting system – one of several features that make it as advanced as it is popular.

Much of the aircraft’s appeal is in the cabin and the field performance. The Phenom 300’s oval cabin cross-section gives it a stand-up center aisle and more headroom than most light jets, along with the lowest cabin altitude and one of the largest baggage compartments in its class. Its short-field capability lets it use airports that many jets cannot, from London City to mountain runways. Modern, efficient, and consistently sought after in charter service, the Phenom 300 is exactly the kind of aircraft that rewards knowledgeable management.

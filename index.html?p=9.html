About Capital Jets

YOUR TRUSTED PARTNER IN PRIVATE AVIATION EXCELLENCE

Capital Jets is built on the values of safety, service, excellence, and reliability. As an ARGUS Platinum-rated operator, we are committed to maintaining the highest standards of operational safety and client service. With an unwavering dedication to passenger well-being and aircraft maintenance, we provide a secure and worry-free travel experience for all our esteemed clients.

Service is at the heart of everything we do. From the moment you engage with us, our team of skilled professionals works to understand and fulfill your requirements – organizing complex itineraries, accommodating specific preferences, and providing personalized assistance at every turn.

We prioritize client safety and maintain strict compliance with all applicable regulations and industry standards. Our ARGUS Platinum rating represents independent, third-party verification of our operations and maintenance. We are also dedicated to enhancing performance while monitoring and managing our environmental impact.

With round-the-clock assistance, any issue or concern is addressed efficiently and without delay. From last-minute itinerary changes to questions about safety protocols, access to knowledgeable professionals at any hour provides invaluable peace of mind.

MISSION

At Capital Jets, we strive to be the premier aviation partner, providing exceptional service and expertise so our clients’ travel experiences are nothing short of remarkable. We deliver personalized charter, management, and sales solutions built around each client’s needs, while upholding the highest levels of safety and reliability.

VISION

We aspire to be a wingman for our clients – providing end-to-end solutions that simplify the complexities of aircraft management and ownership, and make owning an aircraft a genuine peace-of-mind experience.

Our Values

INTEGRITY, EXCELLENCE, AND A COMMITMENT TO YOUR JOURNEY

SAFETY

At Capital Jets, safety is not just a priority – it is the foundation of the company. When you place an aircraft or your family in our care, everything else depends on getting this right. That standard is verified independently through our ARGUS Platinum rating, so the safety behind your flight is confirmed by someone other than us.

RELIABILITY

Reliability is at the core of what we do. Owners trust us to keep their aircraft ready, so we plan maintenance, crew, and scheduling ahead rather than reacting. Whether the trip is business or personal, your aircraft is prepared for the itinerary – ready when you are, on your timeline.

EXPERTISE

Exceptional service depends on the team behind it. Our pilots, technicians, and support staff each hold a defined role in how your aircraft is operated and maintained. Experience shows most in anticipation – resolving issues before they reach the schedule, so trips depart on time.

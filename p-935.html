N106FF

OVERVIEW

N106FF is a 2010 Learjet 60XR — one of the fastest midsize private jets in its class. True to its Bombardier heritage, the Learjet 60XR delivers superior speed and enhanced avionics over the legacy Lear 60, making it an ideal choice for time-sensitive travel. The cabin seats six in leather captain’s chairs, with a bright, elegant interior refined for comfort. Capital Jets has further upgraded N106FF with Starlink Wi-Fi for high-speed in-flight connectivity, and every charter is flown by our ARGUS Platinum-rated crew. Recently painted and refurbished, this Learjet 60XR is our go-to aircraft in the midsize category.

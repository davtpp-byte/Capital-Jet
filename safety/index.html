Safety

METICULOUS AIRCRAFT STANDARDS

At Capital Jets, safety is our top priority. It surpasses all other values that are important to us and we believe that safety should be an integral part of everything we do.

Our rigorous approach to safety begins with our pilots. We understand that the professionalism and skill of the flight crew are foundational to the start of any journey. That’s why our pilots undergo thorough background checks that surpass the requirements outlined by the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA). We handpick experienced aviators who demonstrate exceptional skills, extensive training, and a deep understanding of aviation safety protocols.

PROACTIVE AIRCRAFT MAINTENANCE

Moreover, our commitment to safety is further emphasized by ensuring the security of our clients. Our comprehensive screening processes adhere to local and international aviation security standards, which safeguard your well-being and protect your privacy.

When you work with the Capital Jets team, you can trust that you and your guests will be in safe hands. Our dedication to safety is unwavering and we continually invest in our operation to uphold the efficiency and reliability you’re deserving of.

THIRD-PARTY SAFETY ACCREDITATIONS

Internal standards matter, but they are self-declared. Independent auditing is what turns a safety claim into a verified one.

Capital Jets holds an ARGUS Platinum rating, one of the most demanding safety distinctions in business aviation. Earning it requires more than a clean record on paper. Beyond the historical analysis of our operating record and pilot background verification that underpins every ARGUS rating, Platinum requires an on-site audit conducted at our facility that produces no adverse findings, together with a functioning Safety Management System and a workable Emergency Response Plan.

We are also a WYVERN Registered operator, which places our operational, aircraft, and crew data on file with WYVERN for independent review and client access. Brokers and corporate flight departments consult both registries when vetting an operator, and we maintain our standing in each.

Those independent accreditations are supported by the standards we follow every day. Before every flight, our aircraft undergo thorough inspections to ensure they exceed FAA requirements. Our fleet is maintained by certified technicians in accordance with rigorous industry standards, with proactive maintenance programs and advanced diagnostic technologies used to identify and resolve potential issues before they affect operations. Every aircraft is prepared with the same attention to airworthiness, reliability, and operational readiness that our clients expect.

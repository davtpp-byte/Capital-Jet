Advantages of Buying an Aircraft with Capital Jets

Aircraft Acquisition Backed by an Operator, Not Just a Broker

Buying the right aircraft is only the beginning. Operating it successfully is what protects your investment over time. At Capital Jets, aircraft acquisition is backed by real operational expertise: we charter, manage, crew, and maintain aircraft every day, which gives our clients practical insight that extends well beyond the purchase itself.

Whether you are acquiring your first aircraft or upgrading your current one, we evaluate every opportunity with long-term ownership in mind. From selecting the right aircraft for your mission to supporting maintenance, crew management, charter opportunities, and daily operations, our team stays involved long after closing.

Unlike a traditional brokerage relationship that often ends with the transaction, Capital Jets provides continuous support throughout the life of the aircraft. That operational perspective shapes what we recommend, what we identify before you buy, and how we help protect the value of your investment for years to come.

 

Why Capital Jets

  • Boutique operator, not simply an aircraft broker – a management and charter company that stays with the aircraft long after the purchase closes.
  • Full-service ownership support – management, maintenance, crew, and charter under one accountable team, so nothing is handed off to outside vendors.
  • ARGUS Platinum-rated – independently audited to one of the most respected safety standards in business aviation, with decades of flight and management experience behind it.
  • An approved charter vendor – vetted by leading brokerages, jet card providers, and legacy fractional operators, so an owner’s aircraft can reach charter demand that independently managed aircraft rarely do.
  • In-house maintenance and parts sourcing – dedicated capability that reduces downtime and cost.

  • Transparency and professionalism – clear operating costs, honest guidance, and a single accountable team from acquisition through daily operation.

Buying an Aircraft with Capital Jets

The right aircraft is the one that fits how you actually fly – not the one that looked good on a spec sheet. Before evaluating a single listing, we establish the mission: passenger capacity, typical trip length, preferred airports, runway limits, and annual utilization. Many disappointing purchases trace back to an airplane chosen for the trip flown twice a year rather than the forty that fill the rest of the schedule.

That approach fits both kinds of buyer. Capital Jets assists both first-time buyers and experienced owners upgrading their aircraft.

For first-time buyers, ownership is a significant operational transition, often from charter or fractional use once annual utilization justifies the investment. We simplify that transition – clarifying not only which aircraft fits the mission, but what successful ownership requires once the aircraft enters service.

For existing owners, upgrading usually means running two transactions at once: acquiring a replacement while positioning the current aircraft for sale. Timing, market conditions, and maintenance status all influence the outcome, and our experience lets owners manage both sides with minimal downtime and protected asset value.

Because we may ultimately manage, maintain, crew, and charter the aircraft ourselves, we evaluate every recommendation differently from a traditional broker. We are naturally conservative about aircraft that could become expensive or difficult to operate over time, because we understand the long-term implications of every acquisition decision.

 

Benefits of Buying Through Capital Jets

 

  • A read from real operating experience: A market report shows what a similar aircraft sold for last quarter. We show what it will cost to maintain over the next five years, which inspections are due, and whether avionics upgrades are coming – the factors that separate a valuable asset from an expensive source of surprises.
  • Support that continues after closing: Clients move directly from acquisition into full management, maintenance, crew, and operational support, with the same team throughout – no assembling a roster of separate vendors.
  • Charter opportunities that help offset ownership costs: Through our operating model and charter relationships, qualifying aircraft can generate meaningful revenue while remaining available for owner use.
  • Objective advice: If an aircraft is not right for your mission, we say so before you commit.
  • Protected resale value: Consistent maintenance and complete documentation preserve the aircraft’s market position from the day you take delivery, not the weeks before you list.

Financial Advantages of Buying Through Capital Jets

For owners who charter, the economics are built to return more of the aircraft’s earning potential to you – and to keep recurring cost predictable:

  • Charter revenue across the full fee structure. Through our brokerage and fractional relationships and a floating fleet focused on the Northeast U.S. corridor – one of the highest-grossing business aviation markets in the country – the aircraft earns from more than flight time, including cancellation, non-flight-day, and standby fees.
  • A genuine utilization guarantee. For qualifying aircraft, we guarantee utilization: if we do not fly the contracted hours, we still pay for them. It is a commitment, not a projection.
  • No recurring fees. No management fees, no hangar fees, and competitive fuel pricing passed directly to the owner.
  • No maintenance markup. When third-party work is required, you pay the vendor’s invoice with nothing added on top, and you remain free to choose your own qualified provider and source your own parts, subject to FAA compliance.
  • In-house maintenance and parts sourcing. Dedicated capability that reduces downtime and keeps costs down.

Aircraft Ownership Services

  • Aircraft Management: One experienced team handles scheduling, budgeting, compliance, and daily oversight, so you never coordinate a roster of separate providers.
  • Maintenance: Our in-house capability keeps the aircraft airworthy and available, turning maintenance from a scramble of outside vendors into a managed, predictable part of ownership.
  • Dedicated Parts Sourcing: Our in-house parts department improves component availability, reduces downtime, and helps control maintenance costs.
  • Flight Crew: We recruit, train, and manage crews to the standard of an ARGUS Platinum-rated operator, giving you qualified professionals without the administrative burden of employing them.
  • Charter: Our brokerage, jet card, and fractional relationships put the aircraft in front of real charter demand, turning idle time into revenue that offsets the cost of the operation.
  • Transparent Economics: No management or hangar fees, no maintenance markups, and no owner markup on fuel.
